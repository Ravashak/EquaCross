How every line works

  1. 1

    Read the digits together

    Digits side by side make one number. The tiles 3 4 3 2 2 can read as 34, 32 and 2.

  2. 2

    Find the split

    One number sits on one side of the equals sign, and two numbers sit on the other. You decide where the cuts go.

  3. 3

    Supply the symbol

    Pick plus, minus, times or divide so the line is true. No leading zeros, exact division only, nothing times or divided by 0. Subtraction and division work in either order.

Every board is checked by the game, so you never need to type a symbol.
